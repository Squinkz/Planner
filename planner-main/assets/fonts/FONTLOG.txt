Drafting Small Caps
-------------------
File: DraftingSmallCaps-Regular.ttf
Version: 1.000
Derived from: Architects Daughter, by Kimberly Geswein
Original copyright (c) 2010, Kimberly Geswein (kimberlygeswein.com)
Upstream reference: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/architectsdaughter
License: OFL-DraftingSmallCaps.txt

Planner's bundled version is a modified small-caps variant of Architects Daughter,
renamed to Drafting Small Caps. Its internal family is Drafting Small Caps and
its PostScript name is DraftingSmallCaps-Regular. The modification date and
complete editing history are not recorded here. The upstream copyright and
OFL remain intact. The derivative, including its small-caps changes, remains
under OFL-1.1 and is excluded from Planner's reserved-graphics license.

The separate OFL file preserves the complete license text embedded in the
modified font, with line endings normalized for readability. No glyphs, font
metrics or font binaries were changed when adding these notices.
